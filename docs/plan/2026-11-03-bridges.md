## Proposal

### 1. A humped road (engine)

A straight piece whose road leaves and returns to the floor but stands one
level up in the middle:

```
hump?: { readonly upMm: number; readonly topMm: number; readonly downMm: number }
```

- Both ports at level 0, as on any floor straight; the solver and placement
  see an ordinary straight of the total length.
- The road renders as three segments: up (rise 1), top (level 1), down
  (rise 1, high at start). Floor track crossing under the top is a bridge
  (allowed); floor track under the slopes clashes, as under a ramp.
- Bodies as for any structure: the embankments/abutments as footprints
  [0,0] where the slope is low; the passage under the top stays open.

### 2. Realistic 3D (our own shapes)

Bodies today are boxes. A body gains a `shape`:

| shape | drawn as | used by |
|---|---|---|
| `box` (default) | today's matte box | station footprint, tunnel walls |
| `legs` | four posts and a plate | riser |
| `wedge` | a triangular prism (embankment, abutment) | stone arch bases |
| `arch` | a curved band of given span, rise and depth | stone arch |
| `tower` | two posts joined by cross-beams | suspension bridge |
| `cable` | thin catenary lines | suspension bridge |

And the road follows its levels: a hump's slopes and top are drawn at their
own heights (the 3D builder reads a segment's own level/rise, not only its
ports').

### 3. Parts

- Stone arch bridge (SA): hump up 114 / top ~245 / down 114, total ~474;
  wedge bodies for the embankments, arch body over the passage.
- Suspension bridge (SB): measured next, same shape kinds plus towers.

| D6 | A hump whose top lands above level 1 is a third-level fault, shown like a ramp's | A |
| D7 | Each bridge carries a measured clearance (its opening height); the verdict states it like ramp clearance, independent of the supports note | A |
| D8 | Collision: cautious rectangles from the mesh: each abutment and embankment a floor footprint, only the measured 169.6mm opening open, the raised road at level 1 over it; tests for square, offset and diagonal crossings | A |
| D9 | The road's real height along its length is a 3D-only profile on the hump; every check keeps whole levels | A |
| D10 | `withRise` strips `hump` from stored parts and corrections to bridges are refused; seeds must satisfy up + top + down = length | A |
| D11 | 3D clash markers take the height of the road segment nearest the clash, not the piece's maximum | A |
