Date measuredOn Tools method

Leave a box empty if you did not measure it. A caliper is ideal; a steel rule and a phone photo answer Q1, Q3 and Q4. Only Q2's bend wants care.

Q1 · Does the 72mm-gap loop seat?

Build a closed loop in this order: 4 large curves (E), the long straight (D, 216), 4 large curves (E), the medium straight (A, 144).

Closes with every joint seated?q1.closesYES / NO
If it closes, how does it feel?q1.feelSTRAINED / COMFORTABLE
If not, the leftover gapq1.leftoverGapMmmm
A train runs round without derailing?q1.trainRunsYES / NO

Q2 · How much does one joint give?

Join two straights. Do not force anything to the point of damage.

Pull apart: gap between the end faces at the limitq2.pullApartMmmm
Push together: 0 if the faces touch, else the gap at the hard stopq2.pushStopMmmm
Bend flat on the floor, angle at the limitq2.bendDegdeg
Or: the far end's sideways travelq2.bendSidewaysMmmm
… over this length (the piece's length)q2.bendOverMmmm

Q3 · The 216mm straight (D)

End face to end face, peg includedq3.overallMmmm
Base of the peg to the far faceq3.withoutPegMmmm
Peg neck diameterq3.pegNeckDiameterMmmm
Socket throat widthq3.socketThroatWidthMmmm

Q4 · The ramp (N)

Run: flat on the floor, end to endq4.runMmmm
Along the sloping surfaceq4.slopeMmmm

Q5 · Under a bridge

Tallest vehicle you own, wheels to roofq5.vehicleHeightMmmm
Ramp rise (published 64)q5.riseMmmm
Plank thickness (published 12)q5.plankThicknessMmmm

Q6 · A switch, if you have one

Measure along the centreline, between the two grooves, from the heel (the single end).

Heel to the straight road's endq6.throughMmmm
Heel to the diverging road's end, in a straight lineq6.chordMmmm
That end's distance off the straight road's lineq6.offsetMmmm
