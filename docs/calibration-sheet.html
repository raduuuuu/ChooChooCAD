Three readings the first afternoon raised. Each drawing shows exactly where the tape goes. Measurements are in millimetres.

F1 · The ramp's run, once more (N)

The first reading gave 225 flat and 230 along the slope; a 64mm rise over 225 would need about 234 of slope, so one of the two is off. Re-measure the RUN only.

Stand the ramp on a flat floor. Put a square against each end face, down to the floor, and measure between the two squares along the floor. Do not follow the slope.

Run, end face to end face, peg not countedq4.runMmmm

F2 · Does your tallest train pass under a bridge?

Build one bridge the usual way: a ramp up, the raised track on its supports, a ramp down. Lay a straight underneath, across it.

Measure the air straight up, beside the train. Then push your 55mm train through slowly by hand and watch the roof.
